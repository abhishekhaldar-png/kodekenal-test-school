About Us

A Place Where Every Child Shines

Kodaikanal International School is a co-educational day and boarding school offering education from Nursery to Class XII. Our focus is on holistic development — academics, sports, arts and character building.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide quality education that empowers students to become responsible global citizens.

👁️ Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence that inspires curiosity, creativity and lifelong learning.

Admissions

Admission Process 2026-27

  1. Enquiry — Fill the enquiry form or visit the school office.
  2. Registration — Submit registration form with required documents.
  3. Interaction — Student assessment / parent interaction.
  4. Confirmation — Pay fees and confirm the admission.

Documents Required

  • Birth Certificate
  • Previous Report Card
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card (Student & Parent)
  • 4 Passport Size Photos
