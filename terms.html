Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 2026

By using this website or applying for admission at Demo Kodaikanal International School, you agree to the following terms and conditions. Please read them carefully.

1. Admission Rules

  • Admission is subject to availability of seats and the student meeting the eligibility criteria.
  • All documents submitted must be genuine. Admission obtained with false information will be cancelled.
  • The decision of the school management regarding admission is final.
  • Submitting an enquiry form does not guarantee admission.

2. Fee & Refund Policy

  • Fees must be paid quarterly before the 10th of the first month of each quarter.
  • A late fee will be charged for payments made after the due date.
  • Registration and admission fees are non-refundable.
  • Caution money (security deposit) is refundable at the time of leaving the school after clearing all dues.
  • Fee structure may be revised by the management from time to time.

3. Attendance

  • A minimum of 75% attendance is compulsory to appear in final examinations.
  • Leave applications must be submitted in writing by the parent/guardian.
  • Students absent for more than 15 days without information may have their name struck off the rolls.

4. Code of Conduct

  • Students must wear the proper school uniform every day.
  • Respect towards teachers, staff and fellow students is mandatory.
  • Mobile phones and electronic gadgets are not allowed on campus without permission.
  • Any damage to school property must be compensated by the parent/guardian.
  • Bullying, ragging or misconduct of any kind will lead to strict disciplinary action.

5. Transport & Hostel

  • School transport is optional and charged separately.
  • Hostel students must follow all hostel rules and timings.
  • Parents must inform the school in advance about any change in pick-up arrangements.

6. Privacy Policy

  • Personal information collected through this website (name, phone, email) is used only for school communication and admission purposes.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.
  • Photographs of students may be used on the school website and social media for school events. Parents may opt out in writing.

  • The school is not responsible for the content of external websites linked from this site.
  • Information on this website may be updated at any time without notice.

The school reserves the right to modify these terms and conditions at any time. Updated terms will be posted on this page.
